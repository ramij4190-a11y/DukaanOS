# Phase 6: Field Deployment, Business Validation & Startup Launch

## 1. Executive Summary & Goals
- **Role Transition:** From Production Engineer to Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) & Startup Founder
- **Core Mission:** Deploy DukaanOS with real Indian retail store owners, observe physical store workflows, measure real business metrics (stockout reduction, holding cost savings), and validate product-market fit.
- **Timeframe:** Months 7–9

---

## 2. The 3-Step Field Validation Framework

```
[Step 1: Discovery Interviews] ---> [Step 2: Shadowing & Pilot] ---> [Step 3: Business Impact Audit]
 - 10 Kirana shopkeepers              - 3 Pilot Stores                 - Measure Stockout Rate drop
 - Pain points in reordering          - Parallel run with paper        - Measure Inventory turnover
 - Khata credit reconciliation        - Real-time usability tweaks     - Validate Willingness-to-Pay
```

### Key Business Metrics to Track:
1. **Stockout Rate:** Percentage of customer inquiries unfulfilled due to zero stock. (Target: Reduce by 30–50%).
2. **Dead Inventory Reduction:** Total ₹ capital tied up in zero-velocity SKUs for > 60 days.
3. **Owner Time Saved:** Hours per week spent on manual ledger balancing and calling distributors.
4. **Willingness to Pay:** Conversion from free pilot to paid subscription (Starter ₹299/mo, Growth ₹699/mo).
